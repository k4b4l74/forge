convention; filename-unsafe slashes may be normalized in the saved names.
The saved deck files and import manifest agreed during the read-only audit.

**19 of 22 lists satisfy this custom card-pool, size and copy-limit check.**
This is not a claim about competitive quality, physical printing eligibility,
or correctness of every card implementation. No user deck has been edited.
The authored 60 + 15 fixtures in the playbooks are separate documentation.

The source links preserve attribution supplied by the user; this table audits
the submitted snapshot, not a promise that a linked website's current list is
identical. KabaL Reanimator has no external source. A guide association is
strategic analysis, not a verified classifier result.

## Corrections requiring the deck owner's decision

- **Cam Wall Atog:** keep at most one Strip Mine across main and sideboard for
  this contract. Do not automatically replace the other three with guessed lands.
- **Roger Brand Reanimator:** Earthquake Dragon is outside the allowed editions.
  It is not the Old School card Earthquake, and the name is not silently corrected.
  Choose legal replacements before post-board testing.
- **Carlos Dominique Troll Disk:** the main totals 55. The missing five cards
  cannot be recovered from the label; complete the list before benchmarking.

A 61-, 62- or 64-card main deck is legal here, though consistency differs from
a 60-card fixture. A sideboard may contain fewer than 15 cards. Four Hymn to
Tourach are permitted because Fallen Empires is enabled; do not import another
community's restrictions.

## How to use these associations

Treat high confidence as confidence in the **identity**, not evidence that the
guide wins the matchup. Hybrids need weighted plans rather than one file selected
solely by name. The two Lion Dib Bolt builds, RW list labeled BWR, Mono Green
tempo, RW Robots control and Grixis Rack are especially important ambiguity cases.

Start with actual colors, mana sources, threats and engine combinations.
Preserve an explicit unknown/hybrid outcome if no guide fits well. During ordinary
games, update the opposing read from public or legitimately revealed cards; do
not read its hidden deck to make a perfect classification unless the experiment
explicitly uses open decklists.

Do not apply a fixture's exact sideboard row to one of these lists. Check the
actual 75 or smaller pool, available quantities, legal colors, retained engine
components and opponent evidence first. Keep pre-board and post-board results
separate, and record any owner-approved correction as a new benchmark snapshot.
